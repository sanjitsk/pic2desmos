4 Equation

Open Desmos ↗

Ctrl+Shift+C copies the equation.

  1. Click the first expression line in Desmos.
  2. Press Ctrl/⌘+V.
  3. Wait a few seconds. Optionally hide the grid and axes from the wrench menu.

Very long equations (over ~60k characters) paste fine on desktop but can lag; the phone app is unreliable with huge pastes.

Advanced: multi-expression mode

One parametric equation per stroke (0 ≤ t ≤ 1), handy for recoloring or editing single strokes. Paste all lines at once into the first expression line; Desmos puts each stroke on its own line.
